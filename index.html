The problem

You're extending credit on a hunch.

Every time a buyer asks for thirty or sixty days to pay, you're making a bet. Say no and you lose the order. Say yes and hope for the best. Most suppliers say yes, because that's how you keep customers, and find out too late that the buyer was never going to pay on time, or at all. By then the goods are gone and chasing the money costs more than it's worth.

The fix

Credit Kawach turns the guess into a number.

We pull your buyer's credit history and turn it into a plain risk grade before you extend a single rupee. You still set the terms and make the call. We just make sure you're making it with real information instead of a gut feeling and good intentions.
